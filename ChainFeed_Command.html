Setup

Set Armoury open to when Xanax and points became available for stacking — the consumables window then runs from that moment through the end of the chain, so pre-chain stacking is counted. Leave it blank to track only during the chain. Hits, losses and respect always come from the chain report itself.
Enter a faction-access key and press Load latest chain. If a chain is running it loads live; otherwise it loads your most recent finished chain.

E In is the consumable energy poured in — Xanax plus point refills. E Spent is (Hits + Assists + Losses) × energy per hit, since a loss or assist still burns energy even though it doesn't extend the chain. Xanax and points are read from faction armoury news across the chain window, so only direct faction-armoury use is counted — personal-stash Xanax won't show. The Xan E and Refill E split is in the CSV/Excel export.
